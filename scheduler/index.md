## 3. Overview: No Personal Data Collected
CaSch! Calendar & Scheduler does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The App is designed to function entirely offline. It does not establish an internet connection and does not require one for any of its features. It does not communicate with any external server and does not send any information outside of your device — not to the developer, not to any third party, and not to any cloud service.

The only data the App creates and stores are:

- Appointments you create yourself (name, details, date, time, recurrence, sound setting, cost, subscription status, and optionally a phone number, email address, and website you enter for that appointment)
- App settings you configure (e.g. colors, appearance)

All of this data is stored exclusively on your device using the operating system's standard local storage mechanisms. It never leaves your device.

## 4. Internet Connection
The App does not require or use an internet connection for any of its functionality. It operates fully offline. There are no network requests, no synchronisation, no telemetry, and no analytics of any kind.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate any third-party services. It uses only the Android operating system's built-in local notification and alarm mechanisms to remind you of your appointments; no data is sent to Google or any other party in the process. The App does not use analytics, advertising networks, social media SDKs, cloud storage, or payment processors.

## 6. No Tracking or Profiling
The App does not track your behaviour, location, device identifiers, or usage patterns. No user profiles are created. No cookies or similar tracking technologies are used. No automated decision-making including profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

## 7. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The only data ever stored by the App is what you explicitly enter:

- Appointments (name, details, date, time, recurrence, sound setting, cost, subscription status, and optionally a phone number, email address, and website) — stored on your device for the App's core functionality
- App settings and preferences (e.g. colors) — stored on your device for personalising the app experience

You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data via your device's system settings. The developer has no access to this data and cannot recover it on your behalf.

## 8. Storage Duration
Data stored locally on your device remains there until you uninstall the App or manually clear the App's data via your device's system settings. The developer has no access to this data and does not store it on any server.

## 9. Children's Privacy
The App does not collect any data from any user, including children. It is therefore suitable for use by users of all ages from a data protection standpoint. No age verification is required, as no personal data is processed at any point.

## 10. Your Rights Under Applicable Law (GDPR / UK GDPR)
Although the App does not process any personal data, the following rights are granted to you under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and equivalent national legislation. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting us:

- **Right of access** (Art. 15 GDPR): Since we process no personal data, there is nothing to disclose.
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16 GDPR): No personal data is held by us.
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten") (Art. 17 GDPR): All data resides locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the App.
- **Right to restriction of processing** (Art. 18 GDPR): Not applicable, as no personal data is processed.
- **Right to data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR): Not applicable, as no personal data is processed by us.
- **Right to object** (Art. 21 GDPR): Not applicable, as no personal data is processed.
- **Right to lodge a complaint**: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the developer is based in Germany, the competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg (LfDI BW): https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de. A list of all German data protection authorities is available at: https://www.bfdi.bund.de — You may also contact the supervisory authority in your country of habitual residence.

## 11. Security

We recommend keeping your device operating system up to date to benefit from the latest security protections.
